The shop

A proper barbershop. No rush, no shortcuts.

Brass & Blade opened in 2016 on a quiet corner of old Larnaca, with one chair, one razor and a simple idea: give every man the time a good haircut deserves.

Today there are four of us, but nothing else has changed. Every appointment starts with a conversation, every tool is sterilised between clients, and the coffee is always on.

NikosNikos Charalambous, founder

  • Online booking, 24/7
  • Sterilised tools, every client
  • Coffee, beer or whisky on us
  • Kids and walk-ins welcome
